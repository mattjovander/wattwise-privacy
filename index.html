WattWise Privacy Policy Last updated: October 29, 2026 WattWise ("the app") is an Android app that reads driving and battery data from an OBD2 Bluetooth adapter plugged into your vehicle and shows it to you. It is published by mattjovander ("I", "me"). Contact: wattwise@mattjovander.stream. What stays on your phone The app has no account, no sign-up and no server of its own. These are stored only on your device and are never sent to me: Trips, including distance, time, energy used and regained, cost, and the GPS route Charging sessions you log or the app records Your settings, such as electricity rate, gas price and battery size Readings from your vehicle (speed, battery charge, power, temperatures) while connected Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. The "Delete all saved trips" button in Setup deletes trips at any time. The app asks Android not to back this data up. Permissions and why Bluetooth / Nearby devices: to find and talk to your OBD2 adapter. Location: to record the route of your trips and, on some Android versions, to scan for Bluetooth devices. Location is used while the app is running, including as a notification-backed foreground service during a drive. The app does not ask for background location access. Notifications: to show the ongoing "WattWise is watching for your truck" notification. Internet: to download map tiles and to load ads. Third parties Google AdMob. The app shows ads. Google's advertising SDK may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and information about how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app first asks for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later. See how Google uses data from ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. You can also watch one optional rewarded ad to hide the banner for 24 hours. OpenStreetMap. The map downloads tiles from OpenStreetMap's servers. Those servers can see your IP address and which map area you view, but not your trip data. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy. I do not sell your data, and I do not receive any of the trip or vehicle data described above. Children The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country) and is not designed for them. Safety WattWise is not affiliated with or endorsed by any vehicle maker or by OBD Solutions. Readings are estimates and may be wrong. Do not use the app while driving.
